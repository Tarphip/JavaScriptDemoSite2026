Cave Johnson

Before We Start, this is a gimmick.

Cave Johnson, the founder of "Aperture Science" within the game universe of "Portal". Best known for his lemon rant, he is also known to be a possible sociopath, with many of the experiments he tasks to be preformed considered inhumane. One example being, replacing a test subjects blood with different substances, like peanut butter or gasoline. This evil man, while making very questionable and downright wrong decisions, also lead to the creation of the portal device, a scientific creation that shoots a substance onto once surface, then another substance on another, linking those two substances to create a portal betweem. After years of amazing financal success, the world finally made new more humane laws, which ended up causing the Aperture Science company, to go bankrupt after all of its work was deemed highly unethical. Fianlly, while discovering the fluid that helped create the portal device, it consequently gave him a lethal illness, slowly killing him off until he eventually died.
